# Candidate Lifecycle: Manual Workflow

This document describes the end-to-end candidate lifecycle as a sequence of events, detailing what happens at each step, which apps are involved, and what data is created or moved. No automation or integration details are included; this assumes every action and sync is performed manually.

---

## Core Principle

RecruiterFlow (RF) is the source of truth for all candidate data. LinkedIn URL is the unique identifier for a candidate across all systems.

---

## Event 1: Candidate Search

**App:** LinkedIn Recruiter

Search LinkedIn Recruiter for candidates matching job requirements. Navigate search results using custom hotkeys (AutoHotKey triggers Shortkeys Chrome extension, which executes JavaScript to select page elements for fast navigation: next/previous candidate, open profile, save, archive, page forward/back).

**Data created:** None externally. Candidates identified.

---

## Event 2: Candidate Saved to LinkedIn Project

**App:** LinkedIn Recruiter

Save candidate to a LinkedIn Recruiter Project (one project per job). This acts as a shortlist within LinkedIn before CRM entry.

**Data created:** Candidate added to LinkedIn Project.

---

## Event 3: Candidate Added to RecruiterFlow

**App:** RecruiterFlow (Chrome Extension)

Open the RF Chrome extension on the candidate's LinkedIn profile. The extension scrapes the candidate's LinkedIn data (name, title, company, location, experience, education, profile image, LinkedIn URL) and creates a candidate record in RecruiterFlow. The candidate is also added to the relevant job in RF.

**Data created in RF:**
- Candidate profile (name, title, company, location, experience, education, LinkedIn URL, profile image)
- Candidate added to job

---

## Event 4: Candidate Messaged

**Apps:** LinkedIn Recruiter, TextBlaze, Shortkeys

Navigate to the candidate in the LinkedIn Project messaging view. Use hotkeys to open the message window. TextBlaze loads a message template with variables (candidate name, role details, etc.) and populates the message field. Send the message.

**Data created:** LinkedIn message sent to candidate.

---

### Decision Point: Candidate Replies?

- **No** → Workflow ends for this candidate (or enters a follow-up sequence, out of scope here).
- **Yes** → Continue to Event 5.

---

## Event 5: Candidate Replied

**Apps:** LinkedIn Recruiter, RecruiterFlow (Chrome Extension)

Candidate replies to the LinkedIn message. On LinkedIn Recruiter, use the RF Chrome extension hotkey to update the candidate's job stage from the current stage to **Replied**.

**Data updated in RF:**
- Job stage → Replied

---

## Event 6: Call Booked

**Apps:** LinkedIn Recruiter / Email, RecruiterFlow (Chrome Extension), Google Calendar

If the candidate is interested, send them a calendar booking link. Once they book, a Google Calendar event is created. Update the candidate's job stage using the RF Chrome extension to **Call Booked**.

**Data updated in RF:**
- Job stage → Call Booked

**Data created in Google Calendar:**
- Calendar event with candidate details

---

## Event 7: Candidate Call

**Apps:** Dialpad (Meetings or Phone), Krisp

The call takes place on Dialpad. Krisp detects the meeting via microphone activation cross-referenced with the Google Calendar event, and automatically records the call. Krisp transcribes the recording and generates a meeting outline/notes with speaker labels.

**Data created:**
- Call recording (Krisp, local)
- Call transcript with diarization (Krisp)
- Meeting outline/notes (Krisp)

---

## Event 8: Post-Call CRM Update

**Apps:** LinkedIn Recruiter, RecruiterFlow (Chrome Extension), Krisp

After the call, go to the candidate's LinkedIn profile. Open the RF Chrome extension and:

1. **Add call notes:** Copy the outline generated by Krisp and paste it into the candidate's notes in RF via the extension.
2. **Fill custom fields:** Based on what was discussed on the call, manually select/enter values for:
   - Role type (CSM, AM, AE, SE, SDR, etc.)
   - Tenure
   - Technology vertical (adtech, martech, fintech, cybersecurity, etc.)
   - Sells to (industries)
   - Current compensation
   - Expected compensation
3. **Update contact info:** Add phone number, email if obtained during the call.
4. **Update job stage:** Set to **Shortlist** or **Rejected** based on the call outcome.

**Data updated in RF:**
- Candidate notes (call outline from Krisp)
- Custom field values (role type, tenure, technology, sells-to, current comp, expected comp)
- Contact information (phone, email)
- Job stage → Shortlist or Rejected

---

## Event 9: Follow-Up Email

**App:** RecruiterFlow (Chrome Extension)

Send a follow-up email to the candidate from the RF extension after the call. This typically confirms next steps, thanks them for their time, or requests additional information (e.g., resume).

**Data created:** Email sent via RF.

---

## Event 10: Resume Upload

**App:** RecruiterFlow (Chrome Extension)

Either immediately after the call or when received later, upload the candidate's resume/CV to their RF profile via the extension. Update job stage to **CV Received**.

**Data updated in RF:**
- Resume attached to candidate profile
- Job stage → CV Received

---

## Event 11: Candidate Writeup

**App:** RecruiterFlow (AIRA)

Generate a candidate writeup using RecruiterFlow's AI feature (AIRA). AIRA has access to the full candidate profile, call notes, custom fields, candidate summary, and the job description. The writeup follows a specific format:

- Name
- Location
- Expected Salary
- Notice Period
- LinkedIn
- Reason for Leaving
- Key Highlights (4 bullet points relevant to JD)
- Contact Details

**Data created in RF:** Candidate writeup generated.

---

## Event 12: CV Sent to Client

**Apps:** RecruiterFlow, Email

Send the candidate's formatted resume and writeup to the client. Update job stage to **CV Sent**.

**Data updated in RF:**
- Job stage → CV Sent

---

### Decision Point: Client Interested?

- **No** → Candidate rejected at client stage. Update RF accordingly.
- **Yes** → Candidate progresses to client interview process (out of scope for this document).

---

## Apps Summary

| App | Used At Events | Role |
|-----|---------------|------|
| LinkedIn Recruiter | 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 | Sourcing, messaging, profile access |
| RecruiterFlow (Chrome Extension) | 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 | CRM data entry, stage updates, notes, email |
| RecruiterFlow (AIRA) | 11 | AI-generated candidate writeups |
| TextBlaze | 4 | Message templates with variables |
| AutoHotKey + Shortkeys | 1, 4 | Hotkey navigation and page interaction |
| Google Calendar | 6, 7 | Meeting scheduling, meeting detection |
| Dialpad | 7 | VoIP calls and meetings |
| Krisp | 7, 8 | Meeting recording, transcription, outline generation |
| Email | 6, 9, 12 | Calendar links, follow-ups, client submission |
