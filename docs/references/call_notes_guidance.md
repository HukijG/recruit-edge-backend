## **Screening Checklist**
### **Motivations**
**Description:** "Why is the candidate open to this conversation? Are they actively looking or passive? What is driving any desire to move — dissatisfaction, growth, compensation, something else? Capture their specific reasoning."
### **What They Want in Their Next Role / Company**
**Description:** "What does the candidate say they are looking for in a company, team, culture, or role? Include any stated deal breakers or strong preferences."
### **Salary & Compensation**
**Description:** "When candidates describe their compensation split using ratios like "90/10", "80/20", "70/30" etc, this refers to the percentage split between base salary and variable/bonus — not dollar amounts. For example "it's 90/10" on a $180,000 OTE means approximately 90% base and 10% variable. Always interpret these as ratios unless the candidate explicitly states dollar figures. 
When calculating figures from ratios, round to the nearest $5,000 increment unless the candidate stated an exact figure. Do not infer figures that were not stated or clearly calculable — if only an expected base and a current bonus percentage are mentioned but no current base, do not guess the current base. Just state what was said, e.g. "$180,000 base + 20% bonus". Where both current and expected compensation are discussed, clearly separate them, e.g. "Current: $160,000 base + 20% bonus (80/20 split). Expected: $180,000 base + 20% bonus." If only OTE is stated, present as OTE with the split, e.g. "Current: $190,000 OTE (90/10 split)." 
If current compensation cannot be clearly determined from what was stated, omit it entirely — do not attempt to reconcile conflicting figures or explain why it is unclear. Expected compensation must always be included."
### **Notice Period**
**Description:** "How much notice does the candidate need to give? Any constraints on start date?"
### **Other Processes**
**Description:** "Is the candidate interviewing elsewhere? At what stage? Any competing offers or timelines?"
### **Location & Working Arrangement**
**Description: **"Where is the candidate based? What is their preference or requirement for remote, hybrid, or on-site work? Any relocation considerations?"
